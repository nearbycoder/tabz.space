Tabz

Creates a “Browser bookmarks” board that updates whenever your bookmarks change. Two-way: renaming, editing, adding, moving or deleting links and folders on that board in tabz changes your browser's bookmarks too. Order follows the browser, and groups you make on that board stay in tabz only.

Saves them to an “Archived tabs” board in tabz, then closes them. Pinned and playing tabs are never touched.

Only change this if you self-host tabz.
